# Claude Email privacy policy

Claude Email is a personal tool used only by its owner. It lets the owner read, search and draft email in the owner's own Gmail accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

It reads email only when the owner asks it to, saves drafts only in the owner's own Drafts folder, and never sends email.

Sign-in tokens and any downloaded attachments are stored only on the owner's own computer. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
